How it works

The creator sets up a workflow once. After that, Reflow Studio does the daily work and the creator stays in control.

  1. Connect accountsThe creator signs in to their own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts with each platform's official login.
  2. Create a workflowPick what to make (for example "Daily English Challenge"), the schedule, and which accounts receive it.
  3. Reflow Studio makes the videoThe content is written, checked, drawn and rendered as a video or picture with captions.
  4. PublishEach post is sent to the chosen accounts in the right format. For TikTok, the video goes to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  5. Review the resultsThe Activity page shows every post and its status on each platform.

Accounts page: each platform is connected with its official login and can be disconnected at any time.

How Reflow Studio uses TikTok

Reflow Studio uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to send the creator's own finished videos to their TikTok account.

  1. Log in with TikTokThe creator clicks "Connect TikTok" and signs in on TikTok's own page, approving the permissions shown.
  2. Upload to the TikTok inboxWhen a new video is ready, Reflow Studio uploads it with the Content Posting API (video.upload) to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. The creator posts in TikTokTikTok notifies the creator, who opens the draft in the TikTok app, edits if they want, and posts it themselves.

Permissions we request

user.info.basic to show which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar).

video.upload to send the creator's own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts.

We do not read followers, messages, comments or any other TikTok data. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Questions

Who can use Reflow Studio?

Reflow Studio is the creator's own publishing tool. It runs on the creator's computer and publishes only to accounts the creator owns and connects. There are no public sign-ups.

Is the content original?

Yes. Every lesson, quiz, comic and cartoon is created by Reflow Studio for the creator's channels. It never downloads or reposts other people's videos.

Is AI used?

AI helps write lessons and jokes and provides narration voices. Every item is checked before posting, and videos with AI voices are labelled as AI-generated on each platform.

What data is stored?

Only what is needed to publish: login tokens (encrypted) and information about the creator's own posts, stored on the creator's computer. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I remove access?

Disconnect the account in Reflow Studio, or remove the app's permission in the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook: Settings → Business integrations).
