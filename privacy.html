Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Reflow Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to publish educational videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives permission (through Google OAuth) to upload videos, manage titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and read basic channel and video statistics, for that channel only.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to show the owner how they perform. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Login tokens and video information are stored only on the owner's own computer, with the login tokens encrypted. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which deletes the app's permission immediately.
