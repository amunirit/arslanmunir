Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Reflow Studio ("the app", "we") is a content creation and publishing tool run by Muhammad Arslan Munir. It creates original videos and pictures and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information the app accesses

PlatformWhat we receiveWhy
TikTokBasic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) through Login Kit (user.info.basic); permission to upload videos to the account's inbox as drafts (video.upload).To show which account is connected and to send the owner's own videos to it.
YouTube (Google)Permission to upload and manage videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and to read basic channel and video statistics.To publish the owner's videos and show how they perform.
Facebook and Instagram (Meta)Access to the owner's own Pages and Instagram professional account to publish posts and reels and read basic insights.To publish the owner's content and show growth.

The app does not read followers' personal information, private messages, or contact lists, and it does not access any account that the owner has not connected.

How information is used

Information is used only to publish and manage the owner's own content and to show the owner how it performs. It is never sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Where information is stored

Login tokens and post information are stored on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are encrypted. Information is kept only while an account is connected; disconnecting an account deletes its tokens from the app.

Services the app uses

To create content, the app may send text (never account data) to AI services such as Anthropic and OpenAI, for example to write a lesson or to generate a narration voice. Videos are sent only to the platforms the owner has connected.

TikTok

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. Videos are uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, and the owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). See also the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Some of our content is made for children and is marked "made for kids" where platforms require it. The app itself is not used by children and does not collect information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

You can disconnect any account in the app or remove access from the platform's settings at any time. To ask about or delete any information related to you, email arslan.munir53@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
